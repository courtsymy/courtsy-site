Prices and payments

  • No hidden fees. The price you see on a session — the host’s hourly price × the session length — is the whole price. There is no booking fee, service charge or tax added at checkout, and friends you invite play free.
  • Bookings are free for now. Payments aren’t switched on yet, so bookings are confirmed without a charge and we hold no card or bank details. Checkout shows “Due today: RM0”.
  • Hosts set their own hourly price within a range shown in the app. Once payments start, Courtsy keeps a commission from the host’s side (the percentage is shown to hosts when they list and price a court); it is never added on top of the player’s price.
  • Courts are a real-world service, so when payments start they’ll be taken through a payment provider (card, FPX or DuitNow), not through the App Store. We’ll tell you in the app and update these terms before that happens.
